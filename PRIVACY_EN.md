# Folderito Privacy Policy

Effective as of October 15, 2026.

**Folderito** is a local music player for Android. Developer: Peter Vasiliev
(contact: peter.vasiliev@gmail.com).

## What information is processed

The app **does not collect, store or transmit** any personal data, usage
data, location or any other information about the user. The app contains no
ads, analytics or trackers. Everything it works with stays on the device.

The app reads the user's **audio files** (the `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` permission)
solely to build the library and play music. Playlists (.m3u) created by the
user are saved to the shared music folder on the device and are never sent
anywhere.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` (and `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 9–12) | reading audio files for the library and playback |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`, `WAKE_LOCK`, `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | background playback and the media notification with controls |
| `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS` | only at the user's explicit request — so music keeps playing in the background (audio playback) |

## Children

The app does not collect data about users of any age, including children.
